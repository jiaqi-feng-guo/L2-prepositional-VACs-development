# Data terms

This repository separates three things.

**Code.** The analysis script under `code/` is released under the MIT License (see `LICENSE`).

**Annotations and derived measures.** The annotation layers in the data files (proficiency level, mode, verb, constructional function, error coding, semantic class, and the BCC and CSL measures) are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0). If you use them, please cite the paper. [Confirm this licence choice before release.]

**Underlying corpus text.** The learner sentences come from the Guangwai-Lancaster Chinese Learner Corpus (GLCLC) and are **not** redistributed here. The `Concordance Line` column is masked as `[GLCLC text removed]` in every file. Redistribution and reuse of the corpus text are governed by the GLCLC's own terms of use. Researchers who have obtained GLCLC access can restore the original sentences by joining these files on the `Code` and `ID` fields.
